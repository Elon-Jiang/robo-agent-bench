All five stationery items — the eraser, red pushpin, pencil, marker and sticky-note pad — are now in the orange organizer tray, and I reported the episode as a success.

The final overview image shows each of them inside the tray. The pencil lies diagonally and the sticky pad may be resting partly on it, so the tray is not perfectly neat. I left the coffee mug, phone, screwdriver and tissue box where they were, since they aren't stationery.

Two things went wrong along the way and were recovered: the first eraser grasp failed and knocked the eraser and sticky pad a few centimetres, and the sticky pad stayed wedged in the jaws on its first release until I reopened the gripper fully over the tray.

The run used 12 of the 30 plan turns.
